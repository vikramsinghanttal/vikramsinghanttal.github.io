This paper presents a hybrid CNN-Transformer framework for accurate indoor positioning in 6G networks. By combining convolutional neural networks for local feature extraction with Transformer-based architectures for capturing long-range dependencies, the proposed framework enables robust and precise localization in challenging indoor environments.'
