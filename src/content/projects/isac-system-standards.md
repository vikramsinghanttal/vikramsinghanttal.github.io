This is a body of work I lead at Tejas Networks from 2024 onward, spanning
gNB-based sensing network architecture, sensing-assisted communication
signaling and measurement/reporting procedures, and reference-signal design
for sensing, alongside detection and localization algorithms for monostatic,
bistatic, and fused sensing modes. Documented across a growing patent
portfolio and conference papers, it is the pre-standardization research base
for 3GPP Release 19/20 work on channel modeling and TRP-based monostatic
sensing.

## Selected publications

- **[Unauthorized UAV Detection Using Bistatic MIMO-OFDM ISAC Under Static Clutter](/publications/rahul-isac-comsnets-2027/)** — IEEE COMSNETS 2027. Bistatic MIMO-OFDM ISAC detection framework that suppresses direct-link and static-clutter components in the delay-Doppler domain to flag unauthorized UAVs.
- **[System and Method for Measurements and Reporting for Sensing-Assisted Communication in Integrated Sensing and Communication Networks](/publications/salija-sac_isac-2026.md/)** — Indian Patent Application (2026). Measurement and reporting procedures that coordinate sensing-assisted communication across ISAC networks.
- **[Performance Analysis of UAV Sensing using ISAC-enabled 5G-Advanced and 6G Networks](/publications/vamsi_isac_performance-ants-2026/)** — IEEE ANTS 2026. End-to-end 5G-NR ISAC receiver for weak-target UAV sensing, combining clutter suppression, CA-CFAR detection, and ESPRIT-based ToA/AoA estimation across monostatic, bistatic, and fused sensing modes.
- **[Reference Signal Designation and Sensing Pattern Control for Integrated Sensing and Communication Systems](/publications/salija-rs_sac_isac-2026/)** — Indian Patent Application (2026). Reference-signal designation and sensing-pattern control techniques that coordinate sensing and communication resource use in ISAC systems.
- **[Signaling and Procedures for Sensing-Assisted Communication in Integrated Sensing and Communication Systems](/publications/salija-sap_sac_isac-2026/)** — Indian Patent Application (2026). Signaling mechanisms and operational procedures that integrate sensing information into ISAC communication processes.
- **[System and Method for Signaling and Procedures for Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)](/publications/sunil-ISAC_2026/)** — Indian Patent Application (2026). Signaling framework and protocol procedures for coordinating sensing and communication functionality in ISAC systems.
- **[A Technique for Adaptive Sensing Data Reporting Architecture for Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)](/publications/salija-isac2-2026/)** — Indian Patent Application (2026). Adaptive sensing-data reporting architecture that reduces reporting overhead while meeting network and sensing requirements.
- **[Methods and Systems for Supporting gNB Based Sensing](/publications/akshatha-ISAC-2025/)** — Indian Patent Application (2025). Methods and systems that let a gNB perform, coordinate, and support ISAC sensing operations using wireless signals and network resources.
- **[End-to-End Network System Architecture to Enable Sensing and Communication in Advanced Wireless Networks](/publications/narayana_ISAC-2025/)** — Indian Patent Application (2025). End-to-end network system architecture that integrates and coordinates sensing and communication operations across network entities.
